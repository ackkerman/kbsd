  1. 01

    Download & open

    Unzip kbsd and move it to Applications. This first release is locally signed and isn’t notarized. macOS may show a security warning; review the source and Apple’s opening instructions before proceeding.

  2. 02

    Allow keyboard access

    Open General in kbsd, then Open System Settings. Allow kbsd in Accessibility (called Device Control & Data Access on some macOS versions). No permission is needed for in-app previews.

  3. 03

    Pick your sound

    Choose a sound, set your volume, and get back to whatever you love making.

A few things to know.

Which Macs can run kbsd?

The v1.0.0 download is for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. An Intel build is not included.

Does kbsd record what I type?

No. It uses physical key events to choose a sound, without reading typed characters or storing a key history. Only daily and total counts are saved locally.

Why does it only play inside the app?

Keyboard access hasn’t been granted to the current app. Open General → Open System Settings. If an older build is already allowed, remove that entry, add the current kbsd.app, and click Check again.

Where do the sounds come from?

The switch recordings come from Klack’s sound previews and are included with permission. Their original rights holders retain ownership. See the repository’s asset attribution for details.

What is included in this release?

Seven sounds, press/release feedback, volume and pitch controls, stereo width, modifier and mouse sounds, local counts, and optional launch at login. Head-tracked spatial audio and automatic updates are not included.
